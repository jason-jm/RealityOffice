# Reality Office privacy policy · 隐私政策

The Reality Team · Reality Office 1.3.2 (build 7) · 2026-11-14

## English

The app opens, edits and saves the documents you choose on your device. Shared text and attachments you open are saved as local copies. It stores preferences, recent file paths and recovery drafts locally. The editors do not require an account. The release APK does not request Android's Internet permission.

The built-in file browser can request Android's All files access for browsing shared storage. You can also use the app's own folder or open and save documents through the system picker. A cloud document provider, system spell checker, external browser or device backup may process data according to the service and settings you choose. Copy and paste use the system clipboard.

Slides asks for PICO spatial data access when you open **Present on Wall**. The headset supplies detected wall positions and sizes so that Slides can place and scale the presentation. Declining leaves the presentation on a floating panel. The app processes the geometry on the headset and does not send it to The Reality Team. The system log can include the detected plane identifier and size. You can revoke spatial access in the headset’s app permissions.

The app keeps a local session log and up to ten crash reports. Reports can include the app and OS versions, hardware model, locale, exception details and file paths. Exporting diagnostics creates a ZIP file on your device. You choose whether and where to share it.

To delete local preferences, recent-file history and private diagnostics, clear Reality Office’s storage in the headset settings. Delete exported documents and diagnostic ZIP files in the location where you saved them. If you selected a cloud service or device backup, its own controls apply to those copies. For privacy or support questions, contact The Reality Team through the [project support page](https://github.com/jason-jm/RealityOffice/issues). Issues are public, so keep document contents and private logs out of your message.

## 简体中文

Reality Office 在设备上打开、编辑和保存你选择的文档。从其他应用接收的文字或附件会保存为本地副本。应用在本地记录偏好设置、最近文件路径和恢复草稿。编辑器无需账号。1.3.2 的发布安装包没有申请 Android 的互联网权限。

内置文件浏览器可申请“所有文件访问权”，用于浏览共享存储。也可以使用应用自己的文件夹，或通过系统文件选择器打开文件和保存副本。系统选择器中的云盘、系统拼写检查、外部浏览器及设备备份会按你选择的服务和设置处理数据。复制和粘贴使用系统剪贴板。

仅在 Slides 打开「投影到墙面」时，应用才会请求 PICO 空间数据权限。头显提供检测到的墙面位置和尺寸，以便调整放映面板的位置与大小。拒绝后，仍可在浮空面板上放映。应用仅在头显上处理几何信息，不会将其发送给 The Reality Team。系统日志可能包含检测到的平面标识和尺寸。你可以在头显的应用权限设置中撤销授权。

应用在本地保留会话日志和最多十份崩溃报告，可能包含应用及系统版本、设备型号、语言、异常详情和文件路径。导出诊断会在设备上生成 ZIP 文件，由你选择是否分享及其接收方。

要删除本地偏好、最近文件记录和应用私有诊断数据，可在头显设置中清除 Reality Office 的应用数据。已经导出的文档和诊断 ZIP 可在保存位置单独删除。云服务或设备备份中的副本可在相应服务中管理。隐私和支持问题可通过[项目支持页面](https://github.com/jason-jm/RealityOffice/issues)联系 The Reality Team。该页面公开可见，请勿附上私人文档、日志或联系方式。
