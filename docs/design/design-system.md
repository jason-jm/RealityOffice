# Reality Office design system

Reality Office provides documents, spreadsheets and presentations for a PICO headset. The UI follows the current Microsoft 365
desktop apps (Fluent 2 ribbon, backstage, dialogs, task panes), adapted to PICO SpatialUI. People
read the window from about a metre away and point with a controller ray or a hand, so everything is
larger than on a monitor. The window background is the system glass.

This file is the source of truth for the redesign. Every screen is rebuilt against it, and new UI
starts from it.

## 1. Principles

1. **Office first.** Commands keep the names, grouping and order of Word / Excel / PowerPoint, with
   one look for one kind of control everywhere. Someone who knows Office should never have to hunt.
2. **One surface per path.** Glass is the window. The ribbon is the only app-owned panel in the
   chrome. Content (page, sheet, slide) is the other. Rows, groups and sections are separated by
   space, type and dividers, never by stacked cards or outlines.
3. **Readable at arm's length.** Body and label text 13 sp or larger (never under 12), commands at
   least 32 dp, primary targets 40 dp or more. Text is labelPrimary / labelSecondary on glass;
   status is never shown by colour alone.
4. **Anchored, not pushed.** A dropdown opens a flyout (SpatialPopup) or a menu (Menu) next to its
   button. The page does not jump down. A dialog is centred and modal, with title, content and
   actions at the bottom right.
5. **Calm by default.** At rest, commands are flat icons with labels and no fills. Hover darkens a
   little; checked state is an accent tint, never a black pill.

## 3. Icons

- One family: the 24 x 24 grid with 2 dp strokes and flat ends (PICO ICON 7.0). Generic icons
  come from ICON 7.0 (`res/drawable/ic_*.xml`, copied from the plugin's catalog). Office-specific
  icons (paste, format painter, bold, align, table, chart, borders…) are drawn in the same style.
- Rendered with the theme tint (`LocalContentColor`); colour only for meaning (font colour bar,
  highlight bar, fill bucket swatch).
- No emoji, no Unicode glyphs as icons. Letters that are the Office icon (B, I, U, S) are drawn
  as type with a fixed weight.
- `RibbonGlyph(key)` draws the key's vector (`AeroIcons`: names and the older keys as aliases), or
  the letter icon, or as a last resort the key as text.

use the PicoTheme roles, `controllerHapticFeedback`, `LocalIndication` and the shared `GazeFill`
hover, so they still feel native.
