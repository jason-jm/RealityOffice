# 海边周末 · A weekend by the sea

[下载中文示例（ZIP）](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jason-jm/RealityOffice/main/examples/coast-weekend/coast-weekend-zh-CN.zip) · [Download the English examples (ZIP)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jason-jm/RealityOffice/main/examples/coast-weekend/coast-weekend-en.zip)

两个压缩包各有一份行程、一份预算和一套三页演示文稿，都是[项目首页](../../README.md)截图里实际打开的文件。解压对应语言的压缩包，在头显中打开任意一个文件就能动手改。

Each pack contains the editable documents shown on the [project home page](../../README.en.md). Unzip your preferred language, copy the three files to the headset and open one in Reality Office.

| 编辑器 · Editor | 中文文件 | English file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pages | 海边周末行程.docx | Weekend-Itinerary.docx |
| Sheets | 两人旅行预算.xlsx | Trip-Budget.xlsx |
| Slides | 海边两日.pptx | Two-Days-by-the-Sea.pptx |

**试一试：** 在预算表中，把第一天的餐费（C8）从 76 改为 90。结余会从 119 元变成 105 元。

**Try it:** Change the first day’s food expense in C8 from 76 to 90. The remaining budget goes from 119 yuan to 105.

The Reality Team created these examples for Reality Office. The sample content is available under the project’s [MIT license](../../LICENSE).
