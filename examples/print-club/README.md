# Saturday Print Club

Open the same three English files you see in Reality Office for PICO Space Pro. Unzip the pack on your computer, copy the files into your headset’s Download folder and choose **Open from device or cloud…** on the Reality Office home screen. [Download the example pack (ZIP)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jason-jm/RealityOffice/main/examples/print-club/Saturday-Print-Club.zip)

| File | Try it |
| --- | --- |
| Studio-Run-Sheet.docx | Move the printing demonstration to a later time in the schedule. |
| Materials-Budget.xlsx | Change the paper quantity in B5 from 4 to 5. The order rises from €96 to €108 and the remaining budget drops from €24 to €12. |
| Print-Club-Welcome.pptx | Add a note to the welcome slide, then rehearse the three-slide talk. |

The Reality Team created the sample text and workbook. They are available under the [MIT license](../../LICENSE). The slides include cropped details of Katsushika Hokusai’s *Under the Wave off Kanagawa* (*The Great Wave*), ca. 1830–32. The Metropolitan Museum of Art makes the image available in the public domain as part of its Open Access program. [Collection record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/45434) · [Open Access at The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access)
